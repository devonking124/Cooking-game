Controls

VR (Quest): Grip = grab (large items take two hands) · Trigger = use / click UI · Left stick = smooth move · Right stick = push forward to teleport, flick left/right to snap turn · Turn your left palm toward your face to open the wrist menu, or press Y to pin it in front of you · Point at a distant item, hold grip, and flick your wrist to force-pull it. Change stick functions per hand in Menu → Comfort.

  • WASD move · mouse look
  • LMB hold to grab, release to drop/throw
  • RMB use held item
  • Wheel hold distance
  • R + mouse: rotate held item
  • 1–9 tools, 0 empty hand
  • F force-grab the item you're aiming at
  • Hold T to aim a teleport, release to jump
  • Q/E snap turn
  • M or Tab menu · P perf HUD
